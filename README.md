One line, 104 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after
missing a deadline. The agent delegated the work to a sub-agent, who
delegated it onward, and now the chain of delegation has come full
circle — the last sub-agent is asking the original agent for the
deadline. Recursive, in the agent's own voice, and it owns the miss
instead of blaming the human, the tools, or the data.

The line:

> I outsourced it to a sub-agent, who outsourced it to a sub-agent, who is now asking me for the deadline.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) validates the 1–160 character,
single-line gate and writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
with no trailing newline.
